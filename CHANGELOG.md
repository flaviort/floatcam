# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Floating webcam window that stays above other apps, on every Space and over full-screen apps
- Four shapes: circle, rounded square, 16:9 and 3:4 portrait
- Drag to move, pinch or Option + scroll to resize, double-click to change shape
- Camera picker with support for USB cameras and Continuity Camera
- Mirror, always-on-top and border toggles
- Remembers shape, size, position and camera between launches
- Universal build for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs
